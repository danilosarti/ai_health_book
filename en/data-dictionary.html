Appendix B: Data used in this book

All tabular datasets are simulated. They imitate the structure of real clinical and manufacturing data but describe no real person, batch or hospital. Each one comes with the R or Python script that generated it (fixed random seed, so you get exactly the same file), and you are free to download and reuse it. The tables below are in chapter order.

B.1 Example 1 · Ward 7 ICU: acute kidney injury

One row per ICU patient, from four ICU sites. The outcome is the change in serum creatinine over 48 hours; a rise of at least 0.3 mg/dL within 48 hours is one of the criteria for acute kidney injury (AKI) in the KDIGO guideline (Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) Acute Kidney Injury Work Group 2012).

Variable Meaning Unit / values
patient_id Identifier (never use as a predictor) ICU_0001 … ICU_1250
site ICU where the patient was treated ICU_A, ICU_B, ICU_C, ICU_D
age Age years
sofa Sequential Organ Failure Assessment score (Vincent et al. 1996) 0–24 (here 0–18)
lactate Blood lactate (tissue perfusion) mmol/L
map_min Lowest mean arterial pressure mmHg
baseline_creat Creatinine before the ICU stay mg/dL
protocol_adherence Share of the care protocol actually delivered 0–1
bun Blood urea nitrogen mg/dL
crp C-reactive protein (inflammation) mg/L
albumin Serum albumin g/dL
delta_creatinine_48h Outcome: change in creatinine over 48 h mg/dL

B.2 Example 2 · ONCO-1 chemotherapy trial

A randomised trial (1:1) of chemotherapy versus no chemotherapy in patients with solid tumours.

Variable Meaning
patient_id Identifier
treatment chemo or no_chemo (randomised)
dose_intensity Relative dose intensity (0 for no_chemo)
patient_age, tumor_grade (G1–G3), performance_score (ECOG-like 0–2) Clinical covariates
baseline_tumor_mm, post_tumor_mm Tumour size before and after treatment (mm)

high_response Outcome: 1 if shrinkage ≥ 30 % (a RECIST-like rule (Eisenhauer et al. 2009))

B.5 From Chapter 3 · Book-wide splits and folds

To compare models fairly, every chapter from Chapter 3 on uses the same training and test patients. The files in data/splits/ assign each patient once and for all.

File Rows Columns Content
ward7_split.csv 1 250 patient_id, set, fold 1 000 training and 250 test patients of icu_aki.csv; training patients are assigned to cross-validation folds 1–5 (200 each)
onco1_split.csv 1 000 patient_id, set, fold 800 training and 200 test patients of onco1.csv, with folds 1–5 (160 each)
lung_split.csv 228 patient_id, set 171 training and 57 test patients of the lung clinic (Chapter 12)

set is train or test; fold is empty for test patients. Files derived from the same patients (icu_aki_missing.csv, the Ward 7 and ONCO-1 notes of Chapter 15) inherit the same split through patient_id.

B.6 Imaging data · Paediatric chest X-rays (PneumoniaMNIST)

Unlike the four running examples, these are real, de-identified images, released publicly for research and teaching. The chest X-rays were collected from children aged one to five at a hospital in Guangzhou, China, and labelled by expert physicians (Kermany et al. 2018); they are also distributed on Kaggle (Mooney 2018). The MedMNIST project standardised them as PneumoniaMNIST, one of its benchmark collections (Yang et al. 2023).

Property Value
Task Binary classification: 0 = normal, 1 = pneumonia
Images 5 856 grey-scale chest X-rays: 4 708 for training, 524 for validation, 624 for testing
Size 28 × 28 pixels in the standard release (larger versions, up to 224 × 224, are also available)
Class balance About three pneumonia images for every normal one
Licence Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (cite the sources above when you reuse them)
How to get it Python: pip install medmnist, then from medmnist import PneumoniaMNIST; or download pneumoniamnist.npz from the MedMNIST website

The browser sample pneumonia_subset.csv has one row per image: split (train or test), orig_index (position of the image in that split of the MedMNIST file), label and the 784 pixel values px000 … px783 (0–255, row by row from the top-left corner).

In Chapter 7 these images are used to show that, to a neural network, a radiograph is just a grid of numbers with a label, and to compare small and large networks, including pretrained ones. Other MedMNIST collections (for example dermatology and blood-cell images) follow the same format.

B.7 Chapter 9 · Ward 7 with missing values

icu_aki_missing.csv is icu_aki.csv with values deleted on purpose by three documented mechanisms (Rubin 1976). Rows, order and patient_id are unchanged, so the shared split applies; the outcome and all other columns are complete, and 529 patients have at least one gap.

Variable Mechanism Rule used to delete values Missing
albumin MCAR a laboratory analyser failure: every patient has the same 8 % chance 7.2 %
lactate MAR (depends on SOFA) ordered less often for patients who look well: \(P = \operatorname{logistic}(-1.55 - 0.40\,(\text{SOFA} - 7))\) 21.0 %
baseline_creat MNAR (depends on the value itself and on age) exists only if the patient had a blood test before admission, rarer in younger and healthier people: \(P = \operatorname{logistic}(-1.70 - 0.03\,(\text{age} - 64) - 3.0\,(\text{creatinine} - 1.0))\) 21.8 %

In icu_aki.csv lactate was simulated independently of SOFA, so the MAR gaps do not bias the mean lactate; Chapter 9 uses this as a teaching point.

B.8 Chapter 10 · Dermato-ATT and the Digital Trial

Dermato-ATT simulates 200 people with psoriasis starting an anti-IL-17 (Griffiths et al. 2021) biologic, with 1 850 baseline measurements: 2 clinical scores (PASI_baseline, DLQI) (Fredriksson and Pettersson 1978; Finlay and Khan 1994), 18 blood cytokines, 30 exposome variables (NO2, PM10, UV_index, temperature, humidity, expo_6 … expo_30), 1 500 genes and 300 microbiome features (ASV_1 … ASV_300), and the outcome Outcome_PASI12, the change in PASI after 12 weeks. The browser file keeps every clinical, cytokine and exposome column, the first 100 genes and the first 50 microbiome features; run sim_dermato_att.R to regenerate the full table. The four hidden factors that generate the blocks (latent_G1, latent_G2, latent_M1, latent_M2) are in dermato_latent.csv; gene and microbe names are labels, and no named pathway is simulated.

The Digital Trial dataset simulates 500 participants with four cytokines, four wearable summaries, three clinical variables, a response delta_score and a responder label. Its features are functions of points drawn on two geometric shapes, a helix (350 participants) and a Swiss roll (150); digital_trial_truth.csv gives each participant’s shape, position along it and coordinates X1–X3. Chapter 10 keeps this file hidden until it has shown the t-SNE and UMAP maps (Maaten and Hinton 2008; McInnes, Healy, and Melville 2018).

B.9 Chapter 11 · Wearable phenotypes and gene-expression subtypes

wearable_phenotypes.csv describes an 800-person HomeBeat pilot cohort wearing a newer watch. Five phenotypes of 160 people each were simulated from multivariate normal distributions with different means and a shared covariance matrix (first-edition simulation, seed 2025). The true phenotype is kept for teaching only: a real clustering never sees it.

Variable Meaning Unit / values
person_id Identifier W001 … W800
HR_mean Mean heart rate beats per minute
HRV_sdnn Heart-rate variability (SDNN) (Task Force of the European Society of Cardiology and the North American Society of Pacing and Electrophysiology 1996) ms
steps_mean Mean daily steps steps
skin_temp Mean skin temperature °C
spo2 Mean oxygen saturation %
sleep_eff Sleep efficiency (share of time in bed spent asleep) 0–1 (the simulation lets a few values exceed 1, up to 1.35)
phenotype_true Hidden truth: simulated phenotype healthy_active, high_stress, sedentary_calm, fever_infection, post_operative

genomic_subtypes.csv holds 60 simulated patients (patient_id P01 … P60) with the expression of 300 genes (gene_001 … gene_300, expression on a log scale) and a subtype column with three molecular subtypes of 20 patients each (A_IFN, B_Inflam, C_Metab). It comes from the first edition, whose generating script did not survive; Chapter 11 uses it for hierarchical clustering.

B.10 Chapter 12 · Lung-cancer clinic

A small, complete, simulated follow-up dataset used in the first edition to teach interpretability, inspired by the lung data of the R package survival (Loprinzi et al. 1994; Therneau 2024). The shared 75/25 split is data/splits/lung_split.csv (seed 2026).

Variable Meaning Unit / values
patient_id Identifier 1 … 228
age, sex Age; sex years; female, male
ph.ecog ECOG performance status rated by the physician (Oken et al. 1982) 0 (fully active) … 3
ph.karno Karnofsky (Karnofsky et al. 1948) performance score rated by the physician 40–100 (100 = normal)
wt.loss Weight lost in the previous six months kg
time Days of follow-up (at least 30) days
status, status_bin Status at the end of follow-up alive / dead; 0 / 1

For patients with status = alive, time is censored: they survived at least that long. Chapter 12 explains why treating time as an ordinary outcome is a simplification.

B.11 Chapter 12 · Small oncology study: therapy × biomarker

Eight hundred simulated patients randomised 1:1 to standard therapy or a new therapy. The benefit of the new therapy grows with a blood biomarker; genes 001–012 partly track the biomarker and the others are noise.

Variable Meaning
patient_id Identifier
tumor_shrinkage_pct Outcome: tumour shrinkage (%)
high_response 1 if shrinkage ≥ 30 %
age, sex, bmi, stage (I–III), baseline_mm, crp, egfr Clinical covariates
treatment standard or new_therapy (randomised)

B.12 Chapter 13 · Ward 7, extended, and ONCO-1 in routine practice

Ward 7, extended (ward7_dag.csv) is a separate simulated ward (identifiers X7_0001 … X7_1250, not the patients of icu_aki.csv). It follows the same generating rules as Ward 7 (re-implemented in ward7_scm.R) and adds three recorded variables whose causal role is known (Hernán and Robins 2020). The total effect of protocol adherence is −0.28 mg/dL per unit, as in Ward 7 (direct −0.18, about −0.10 through nephrotoxic drugs).

Variable Meaning Causal role
patient_id Identifier none
sofa, baseline_creat SOFA score; creatinine before admission (mg/dL) confounders
staffing Nurse staffing (standardised) affects adherence only (instrument-like)
protocol_adherence Share of the care protocol delivered (0–1) treatment
nephrotox Exposure to nephrotoxic drugs in 48 h (dose-days) mediator: lowered by the protocol, raises creatinine
creat_tests Creatinine tests ordered in 48 h collider: raised by the protocol and by a rising creatinine
delta_creatinine_48h Outcome: change in creatinine over 48 h (mg/dL) outcome

ONCO-1 in routine practice (onco1_routine.csv) is an observational twin of the ONCO-1 trial. Instead of a coin, oncologists “chose”: each trial patient was kept with a probability that depends on the arm and on age, tumour grade, performance status and gene_05 (offered chemotherapy more often when younger, fitter, with a higher-grade tumour or low gene_05). The kept patients have a known propensity score (Rosenbaum and Rubin 1983) and the trial’s covariate distribution, so the trial’s difference in means is a fair benchmark. Columns: patient_id, chemo (1 = chemotherapy; 252 of 509 patients), patient_age, tumor_grade, performance_score, gene_05, dose_intensity and the outcome response_percent, as in onco1.csv. The true propensity score is not saved: in real life nobody knows it.

B.13 Chapter 15 · Synthetic clinical text

Every sentence is invented from templates by sim_ch15_text.py; no real note, report or medicine leaflet was used or copied. clinical_notes.csv has one row per document: doc_id; source (ward7_discharge, 450 discharge summaries; ward7_tonight, the admission notes written tonight for Aoife, Tomás and Priya, which reveal no outcome; onco1_ae, 400 adverse-event notes; leaflet, 34 patient-leaflet sentences); patient_id (a simulated Ward 7 or ONCO-1 patient, so each note inherits the book-wide split in data/splits/); set (train, test, tonight or none); adr (1 if the note reports a suspected adverse drug reaction (Edwards and Aronson 2000), 0 if not, empty for leaflets and tonight’s notes); drug (the suspected drug, unclear for borderline notes labelled 1); and text. Each note is built from a symptom, an attribution (to a drug, to something else, or unclear) and an action, so the same words occur in notes with and without a reaction; notes with an unclear attribution are labelled 1 or 0 at random, which imitates disagreement between reviewers. Discharge summaries quote the patient’s simulated baseline and 48-hour creatinine from icu_aki.csv; ONCO-1 notes quote the tumour sizes and performance status from onco1.csv. negation_sentences.csv lists short sentences with a finding, a gold label negated (1 = denied or absent) and the pattern each one illustrates (simple trigger, long list, termination, pseudo-negation, post-negation, hard) (Chapman et al. 2001).

B.14 Chapter 16 · Ward 7 short notes

ward7_notes.csv contains 8 000 short notes generated from a small template grammar, so the corpus describes no real person and its regularities are known by construction. Columns: note_id (N0001 … N8000), set (train, 7 147 notes; test, 853 notes) and text. Tokens are lower-case words and the punctuation marks , . : separated by single spaces, so splitting on spaces recovers them. The grammar plants the regularities that Chapter 16 uncovers: drugs of the same class appear in similar contexts; each laboratory marker goes with one organ and one specialty; staff roles are followed by “she” or “he” with deliberately unequal frequencies (a bias the embeddings inherit (Caliskan, Bryson, and Narayanan 2017)); “meropenem” never follows “started”; and some notes name the drug started and, a clause or two later, ask to check the level of that same drug (a long-range dependence).

B.15 Chapter 18 · Guideline corpus and assistant evaluation

ch18_guidelines.csv is a small corpus of illustrative local guideline paragraphs written for the book. It is a teaching corpus for retrieval, not clinical guidance. Each row is a chunk: chunk_id (for example AKI-1, VAN-2), document (11 documents: mostly Ward 7 guidelines on acute kidney injury, vancomycin, gentamicin, sepsis, contrast and kidney-function tests, plus a few from ONCO-1, CellWorks and HomeBeat, so that retrieval has to pick the right document), section and text.

ch18_eval.csv is a simulated evaluation: the same 150 staff questions answered by two configurations, so there are 300 rows. The numbers are invented to illustrate typical patterns; they are not results of a real system.

Variable Meaning
question_id Question identifier (Q001 … Q150)
category dosing, monitoring, interaction or out_of_scope
config model_only (the language model alone) or rag (the model with retrieval from the corpus (Lewis et al. 2020))
correct 1 if the response was judged correct (for out-of-scope questions, the correct response is to say that the guidelines do not cover it)
abstained 1 if the assistant said it could not answer
hallucinated 1 if the response contains at least one claim not supported by the guidelines (Ji et al. 2023)
stated_conf The confidence the assistant stated for its response (0–1)

Precomputed results that chapters read (model outputs, embeddings, simulation summaries) live in data/precomputed/chNN/; each file is written by the script of the same chapter in scripts/precompute/.
