Appendix D — Notation

One symbol per idea, throughout the book. Vectors are bold lower-case (\(\mathbf{x}\)), matrices bold upper-case (\(\mathbf{X}\)), estimates carry a hat (\(\hat f\)). The first table lists the symbols used everywhere; the next ones list, chapter by chapter, the symbols each chapter adds (each chapter also opens with its own “notation at a glance” box). A few letters unavoidably carry more than one meaning, because the literature of each field uses them; the last table lists them, with the chapter where each meaning applies.

D.1 Symbols used throughout the book

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(n\) number of instances (rows) Ch. 1
\(p\) number of predictors (columns used as inputs) Ch. 1
\(i\) index of an instance, \(i = 1, \dots, n\) Ch. 1
\(\mathbf{x}_i = (x_{i1},\dots,x_{ip})\) predictors of instance \(i\) Ch. 1
\(y_i\) outcome of instance \(i\) Ch. 1
\(f\) true, unknown relationship in \(y = f(\mathbf{x}) + \varepsilon\) Ch. 1
\(\varepsilon\) error term Ch. 1
\(\hat f\), \(\hat y\) estimated model, prediction Ch. 1
\(\mathcal{A}\) set of alternatives of a decision Ch. 1
\(\mathcal{K}\) state of knowledge of the decision-maker Ch. 1
\(a\) a chosen alternative Ch. 1
\(p_{\text{AKI}}\) probability of acute kidney injury Ch. 1
\(V\) number of folds in cross-validation Ch. 1
